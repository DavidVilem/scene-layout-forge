# Verify one image

Before rendering, validate declared counts, connections, supported contacts and entrances. Review the guide's actual projection and visibility, not only its JSON report.

After artistic generation, inspect the whole image for composition, depth, architectural density, paper tone and required count readability. Inspect pump connections, elbows, stairs/landings, platform supports, workers' feet and label arrowheads at useful zoom. Count the actual generated objects again. Classify required routes as visible-consistent, occluded-unverified or contradicted.

Use the [cross-subject style review](style-review.md) and the [evaluation rubric](../evals/rubric.json). A numerical test result cannot satisfy an artistic criterion. Correct local defects up to the entrypoint's limit; report remaining defects or uncertainty. Do not quietly switch to a calculated guide when an artistic PNG was requested.

Always retain contracts, guides, prompts, source scripts, variants and independent reviews in project history, including failures. Select one final PNG; do not automatically delete intermediates. Keep runtime files outside the distributable skill.
