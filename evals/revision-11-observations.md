# Revision 2026-11-13.11: observed scope

The release separates design exploration, physical realization, display projection and artistic pixels. It does not promise a universal CAD solver or error-free image generation.

## Executed checks

- 325 script tests and 3 relocation/package tests passed on the release source.
- Ten procedural examples passed their applicable checks and deliberate negative controls. Older examples retain partial coverage; they have not silently become strict scenes.
- The six-domain, four-seed design matrix exercised 24 selection runs and 96 abstract candidates. Each domain produced two selected topology fingerprints across those four seeds. These are graph trials, not 24 physical buildings or artistic images.
- Package hygiene and skill frontmatter validation passed. No images, personal paths or external reference artwork are bundled.
- Nine real-renderer tests cover drawing projection, vanishing families, depth scaling, tilted verticals and unsupported lens modes.

## Independent forward review and corrections

A short request for a mountain bunker with six connected levels exposed two failures not found by numerical preflight alone: the example had imposed its own hub family as a user constraint, and the initial terrain was only a pair of contextual walls. The final source explores five families without that constraint, selects a hub for its documented seed, and includes a complete contextual mountain with a real cavity and a separate display section.

A subsequent review found a 0.20 m gap between lower floor bases and the modeled geological layer. Explicit foundation pads and support contacts now close that gap. The building bases are no longer declared ground roots; a raised-pad regression rejects a missing contact. The outer geological boundary remains a conceptual support root, not a load or geotechnical simulation.

The final guide contains 849 registered pieces, six levels and five connecting flights. Physical checks, realized graph mapping, view evidence and finishing preflight passed. Its artistic trial was generated from the written style and current calculated guide, followed by one targeted finishing revision for foundation depiction and quieter handwritten annotations. The final image visibly retains six occupied levels, five stair flights and five English leaders, with richer room-specific furniture and geological context. No private style image was used.

## Limits retained

- The bunker materializer currently implements the selected hub family only. Other selected families fail explicitly and require their own materializer; abstract exploration is not proof that those alternatives have been physically built.
- Room programs and semantic roles remain builder declarations checked against allocations and registered parts; the validator is not a semantic image recognizer.
- Curved/hollow collision, arbitrary rotated support and novel functional assemblies may be unsupported. Critical unknowns cannot be counted as passed.
- Ground roots are declared modeling boundaries. Explicit visible terrain requires actual foundation contact, not a root flag to disguise a gap.
- Some rear rooms, return landings and service connections are occluded in the final image. They remain visually unverified. Image generation may alter small fittings and furniture; physical validation does not certify generated pixels.
- Rock stability, loads, ventilation performance, fire safety, code compliance and dynamic operation were not simulated.

Preserve these scopes when reporting improvement. A selected attractive sample does not demonstrate guaranteed diversity or identical detail across clients.
