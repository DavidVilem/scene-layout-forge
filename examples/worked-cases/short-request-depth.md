# Short requests across domains

"A launch viewed from mission control": plan differentiated console groups, operator circulation and equipment/service spaces before screen microdetail. Develop outside launch infrastructure independently of the room. State fictional telemetry as illustrative, not measured flight data. Avoid deriving real site distances from perspective alone.

"Show inside a reactor with a cutaway": select and disclose a conceptual reactor family; consult primary educational documentation for system relationships. Keep a clear local section edge and distinct circuits. Design a relevant enclosure rather than reuse a generic tank hall. Hidden internals and operational fidelity remain unverified; do not imply the cutaway is an actual opening during operation.

"A water treatment plant": develop equipment, access platforms, structural bays, service routes and occupied maintenance zones in several depths. Preserve passages and legible principal connections. More bolts on three isolated cylinders do not substitute for these secondary systems.

These cases define expected planning decisions, not completed visual tests or fixed scene templates.
