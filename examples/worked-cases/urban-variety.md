# Worked case: varied urban district

Brief: a dense district with an aerial view and many tall buildings.

Plan related building families with distinct proportions, heights, setbacks, podiums and roof uses. Vary block dimensions and open-space placement according to circulation while preserving connected streets. Shared construction modules within a building are intentional repetition; identical towers and plazas pasted across the district are not meaningful variety.

Place taller/slender groups and lower transition buildings to keep requested streets and label destinations visible. Plan garden, service-room and terrace roofs separately with access and supported equipment. Grounding comes from street continuity, contact and scale; do not automatically add a floating rectangular base. Choose aerial angle freely unless specified. Maintain any explicit building count in final pixels; background towers count too unless the user distinguishes context from the counted group.

Review silhouettes at fit view and corners/floor bands at native scale. Reject random scale changes, roof equipment overlap, inaccessible terraces, invented building names or extra towers added solely to fill the frame. This is a written reasoning case, not a city generator or code-compliance model.
