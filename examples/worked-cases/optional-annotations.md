# Same richness, different annotation intent

These are fresh-session behavioral probes, not validated vehicle/urban adapters.

| Short request | Annotation policy | Workflow expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Draw a tracked exploration vehicle crossing a dry gully, seen from behind. | none, zero labels/arrows | Start history, adapt actual track/terrain geometry and support evidence, inspect guide, then finish. Unsupported essential contact geometry blocks approval. |
| Draw a city skyline seen from a rooftop. | none | Retain foreground roof construction, varied building silhouettes, street connections and atmospheric depth without adding callouts. |
| Draw a city skyline seen from a rooftop, with five English annotations. | requested, exactly five | Same drawing richness and free viewpoint; five actual visible named targets. |
| Draw a crane in a shipyard. Save only the final PNG. | none | One selected image in delivery folder; original brief, guides, source, prompts and revisions remain in history automatically. |
| Draw an annotated maintenance study of an electric winch. | requested, a few relevant labels | Respect transmission/support checks and retain history; do not infer a fixed factory layout. |

Test a new chat without images/history. Inspect the recorded preparation receipt and actual final PNG separately. An attractive output with no guide or history fails the process probe. An unannotated picture with less construction detail fails the style probe. Do not count removal of labels as a license to remove architecture, scenery or material cues.
